Frequently asked questionsSu'aalaha badanaa la isweydiiyo

How much money do I need to start?

Immisa lacag ayaan u baahanahay inaan ku bilaabo?

You can start with as little as $50–$200 for basic ads and samples if you begin on free social platforms rather than a paid store builder.

Waxaad ku bilaabi kartaa in ka yar $50–$200 xayaysiisyo aasaasi ah iyo tusaale haddii aad ku bilowdo barxadaha bulshada ee bilaashka ah halkii aad dukaan lacag ah ka dhisi lahayd.

Is dropshipping legal?

Dropshipping-ku ma sharci baa?

Yes — it's a legitimate retail model used by major companies worldwide. Just be transparent with customers about delivery times and honor your return/refund policy.

Haa — waa qaab ganacsi sharci ah oo ay isticmaalaan shirkado waaweyn adduunka oo dhan. Kaliya la daacad ahaw macaamiisha xagga waqtiga gaarsiinta oo dhawra siyaasaddaada soo celinta.

How long until I see profit?

Immisa waqti ka hor inta aan faa'iido arko?

Most sellers need 4–12 weeks of testing products and ads before finding a consistent winner. Treat the first month as learning, not guaranteed income.

Inta badan iibiyayaasha waxay u baahan yihiin 4–12 toddobaad oo ay ku tijaabinayaan alaabta iyo xayaysiisyada ka hor inta aysan helin mid joogto ah. U qaado bisha koowaad sida barasho, ma aha dakhli la hubo.

Do I need a business license?

Ma u baahanahay shatiga ganacsiga?

It depends on your country and how you get paid. Check local regulations before scaling up — this guide is educational, not legal advice.

Waxay ku xiran tahay wadankaaga iyo sida lagu bixiyo lacagta. Hubi sharciyada maxalliga ah ka hor inta aad ballaarin. Buugani waa mid waxbarasho, maaha talo sharci ah.

What if a customer complains a package never arrived?

Maxaan sameeyaa haddii macmiil ka cabanayo alaab aan gaarin?

Track the order with your supplier immediately, communicate honestly with the customer, and offer a refund or reship per your policy. Fast, honest handling protects your reputation.

Isla markiiba la soco dalabka alaab-siiyaha, si daacad ah ula xiriir macmiilka, oo bixi lacag-celin ama dib-u-dir sida siyaasaddaada tahay. Xamaasad iyo daacadnimo ayaa ilaalisa sumcaddaada.
