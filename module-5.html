The Somalia opportunityFursadda Soomaaliya

Mobile money is the real payment infrastructureLacagta mobilku waa kaabayaasha lacag-bixinta dhabta ah

Roughly 89% of Somalis use mobile money, versus under 9% with a bank account. Leading services: EVC Plus (Hormuud), Zaad (Hormuud/Telesom), e-Dahab (Dahabshiil), Sahal (Golis), and Premier Wallet.

Ku dhawaad 89% Soomaalida ayaa isticmaala lacagta mobilka, marka loo eego 9% oo bangi haysta. Adeegyada hormuudka ah: EVC Plus (Hormuud), Zaad (Hormuud/Telesom), e-Dahab (Dahabshiil), Sahal (Golis), iyo Premier Wallet.

Social commerce over traditional storesGanacsiga bulshada wuxuu ka fiican yahay dukaamada dhaqameed

Much of Somali online commerce happens through WhatsApp catalogs and Facebook Pages, not standalone websites. A well-run WhatsApp Business + Facebook Page can outperform a website early on.

Inta badan ganacsiga Soomaaliyeed ee internetka wuxuu ka dhacaa WhatsApp iyo Facebook, ma aha websitayaal gaar ah. WhatsApp Business + Facebook Page oo si fiican loo maamulo ayaa ka fiican kara website bilowga.

Logistics realityXaqiiqda gaarsiinta
