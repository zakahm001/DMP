FoundationsAasaaska

What is dropshipping?Waa maxay dropshipping-ku?

Dropshipping is a way of selling products online without ever holding the inventory yourself. You run the online store and handle marketing and customer service. When a customer buys something, you forward that order to a supplier, who packs and ships it directly to the customer — never passing through your hands.

Dropshipping waa hab lagu iibiyo alaabta internetka iyadoon aadan marnaba haysan bakhaarka. Waxaad maamashaa dukaanka online-ka ah, waxaadna maamashaa suuqgeynta iyo adeegga macaamiisha. Marka macmiil wax iibsado, dalabka waxaad u gudbisaa alaab-siiyaha, kaas oo si toos ah ugu diri macmiilka — mar walba iyada oo aan gacantaada marin.

You make your profit from the difference between what the customer pays you and what the supplier charges you.

Faa\'iidadaada waxay ka timaadaa farqiga u dhexeeya waxa macmiilku ku bixiyo iyo waxa alaab-siiyuhu kaa qaado.

In one sentence:Hal jumlad: you are the storefront and the marketer; the supplier is the warehouse and the delivery service. adigu waxaad tahay dukaanka iyo suuqgeeyaha; alaab-siiyuhuna waa bakhaarka iyo adeegga gaarsiinta.

Why people choose this modelSababta dadku u doortaan qaabkan

  • Low starting cost — no need to buy stock upfront or rent a warehouse.
  • Low risk — you only pay the supplier after a customer has already paid you.
  • Flexible — can be run from a phone or laptop, from anywhere.
  • Wide product range — you can test many products quickly without owning any of them.
  • Kharash bilow oo hooseeya — uma baahnid inaad hore u iibsato alaab ama aad kiraysato bakhaar.
  • Khatar yar — alaab-siiyaha kuma bixisid ilaa macmiilku horta ku bixiyo.
  • Dabacsanaan — waxaad ka shaqayn kartaa taleefan ama laptop, meel kastaba.
  • Alaab kala duwan oo badan — waxaad si degdeg ah u tijaabin kartaa alaab badan iyadoon mid ka mid ahi kuu lahayn.

What it is notWaxa uusan ahayn

  • It is not passive income — running ads, answering customers, and managing suppliers is real, ongoing work.
  • It is not guaranteed profit — margins per item are usually thin, so volume and repeat customers matter.
  • It is not instant — building trust and a reliable supplier chain takes weeks to months.
  • Maahan dakhli aan shaqo lahayn — xayaysiisyada, ka jawaabidda macaamiisha, iyo maaraynta alaab-siiyayaasha waa shaqo dhab ah oo joogto ah.
  • Maahan faa\'iido la hubo — faraqa alaab kastaa inta badan waa yar yahay, marka tirada iyo macaamiisha soo noqnoqda ayaa muhiim ah.
  • Maahan mid degdeg ah — dhisidda kalsoonida iyo silsilad alaab-siiye la isku halayn karo waxay qaadataa toddobaadyo ilaa bilo.

The five building blocks you needShanta walxood ee aad u baahan tahay

  1. A niche — a focused product category rather than "everything."
  2. A supplier — a factory, wholesaler, or platform (AliExpress, CJdropshipping, local wholesaler) that ships when you order.
  3. A storefront — a website or a social media page/WhatsApp catalog where customers order.
  4. A payment method — a way for customers to pay you, and for you to pay suppliers.
  5. Marketing — ads, content, or word of mouth that brings customers in. Without this, nothing else matters.
  1. Niche (qayb gaar ah) — nooc alaab oo la xaqiijiyay, ma aha "wax walba."
  2. Alaab-siiye — warshad, tuujaar, ama barxad (AliExpress, CJdropshipping, tuujaar maxalli ah) oo kuu diri karta marka aad dalbato.
  3. Dukaan — website ama bog bulsho/WhatsApp oo macaamiishu ka dalban karaan.
  4. Hab lacag-bixin — hab macaamiishu kuugu bixiyaan, iyo hab aad u bixiso alaab-siiyaha.
  5. Suuqgeyn — xayaysiis, waxqabad, ama af-ka-af oo macaamiil keena. La'aantiisa, wax kale ma qiimo lahan.

Local FocusDiirad Maxalli ah

The Somalia opportunityFursadda Soomaaliya

Mobile money is the real payment infrastructureLacagta mobilku waa kaabayaasha lacag-bixinta dhabta ah

Roughly 89% of Somalis use mobile money, versus under 9% with a bank account. Leading services: EVC Plus (Hormuud), Zaad (Hormuud/Telesom), e-Dahab (Dahabshiil), Sahal (Golis), and Premier Wallet.

Ku dhawaad 89% Soomaalida ayaa isticmaala lacagta mobilka, marka loo eego 9% oo bangi haysta. Adeegyada hormuudka ah: EVC Plus (Hormuud), Zaad (Hormuud/Telesom), e-Dahab (Dahabshiil), Sahal (Golis), iyo Premier Wallet.

What this means for us:Waxa ay noo micnaysaa: checkout should be built around mobile money and cash-on-delivery first — not international cards. lacag-bixinta waa in lagu dhisaa lacagta mobilka iyo cash-on-delivery marka hore — ma aha kaadhadhka caalamiga ah.

Social commerce over traditional storesGanacsiga bulshada wuxuu ka fiican yahay dukaamada dhaqameed

Much of Somali online commerce happens through WhatsApp catalogs and Facebook Pages, not standalone websites. A well-run WhatsApp Business + Facebook Page can outperform a website early on.

Inta badan ganacsiga Soomaaliyeed ee internetka wuxuu ka dhacaa WhatsApp iyo Facebook, ma aha websitayaal gaar ah. WhatsApp Business + Facebook Page oo si fiican loo maamulo ayaa ka fiican kara website bilowga.

Logistics realityXaqiiqda gaarsiinta

  • International shipping into Somalia can be slow — set honest delivery expectations.
  • Consider bulk-importing popular items into a local hub for faster fulfillment.
  • Partner with a trusted local courier for last-mile delivery.
  • Rarida caalamiga ah ee Soomaaliya way gaabsan kartaa — si daacad ah ula socod filashada macmiilka.
  • Ka fiirso soo dejinta tirooyinka badan ee alaabta caanka ah bakhaar maxalli ah.
  • La shaqee kuur gaarsiin la isku halayn karo oo maxalli ah.

Why this can work in our favorSababta ay noo shaqeyn karto

  • Large, young, mobile-first population
  • Growing diaspora remittance economy
  • Relatively low competition
  • Strong word-of-mouth culture

Custom t-shirts or mugs with local designs — printed and shipped only after an order, no inventory risk.

Funaanado ama koobabka leh naqshado maxalli ah — oo kaliya la daabaco kadib dalabka, khatar bakhaar ma jirto.

How much money do I need to start?

Immisa lacag ayaan u baahanahay inaan ku bilaabo?

You can start with as little as $50–$200 for basic ads and samples if you begin on free social platforms rather than a paid store builder.

Waxaad ku bilaabi kartaa in ka yar $50–$200 xayaysiisyo aasaasi ah iyo tusaale haddii aad ku bilowdo barxadaha bulshada ee bilaashka ah halkii aad dukaan lacag ah ka dhisi lahayd.

Is dropshipping legal?

Dropshipping-ku ma sharci baa?

Yes — it's a legitimate retail model used by major companies worldwide. Just be transparent with customers about delivery times and honor your return/refund policy.

Haa — waa qaab ganacsi sharci ah oo ay isticmaalaan shirkado waaweyn adduunka oo dhan. Kaliya la daacad ahaw macaamiisha xagga waqtiga gaarsiinta oo dhawra siyaasaddaada soo celinta.

How long until I see profit?

Immisa waqti ka hor inta aan faa'iido arko?

Most sellers need 4–12 weeks of testing products and ads before finding a consistent winner. Treat the first month as learning, not guaranteed income.

Inta badan iibiyayaasha waxay u baahan yihiin 4–12 toddobaad oo ay ku tijaabinayaan alaabta iyo xayaysiisyada ka hor inta aysan helin mid joogto ah. U qaado bisha koowaad sida barasho, ma aha dakhli la hubo.

Do I need a business license?

Ma u baahanahay shatiga ganacsiga?

It depends on your country and how you get paid. Check local regulations before scaling up — this guide is educational, not legal advice.

Waxay ku xiran tahay wadankaaga iyo sida lagu bixiyo lacagta. Hubi sharciyada maxalliga ah ka hor inta aad ballaarin. Buugani waa mid waxbarasho, maaha talo sharci ah.

What if a customer complains a package never arrived?
