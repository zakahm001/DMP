Your role — day to dayDoorkaaga — maalin walba

DailyMaalin kasta

  • Check and confirm new orders; place matching orders with the supplier
  • Reply to customer messages (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, store chat)
  • Track packages in transit and flag delays
  • Post or schedule at least one piece of content
  • Hubi oo xaqiiji dalabyada cusub; u dir dalabyada alaab-siiyaha
  • Ka jawaab fariimaha macaamiisha (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, dukaanka)
  • La soco xirmooyinka socda oo calaamadi dib u dhac
  • Dir ama jadwali ugu yaraan hal qayb waxqabad ah

WeeklyToddobaad kasta

  • Review which products are selling well vs. not, and why
  • Check ad performance and adjust budget
  • Research 2–3 new potential products
  • Respond to reviews/feedback and handle returns
  • Dib u eeg alaabta si fiican u iibisa iyo tan aan u iibin, iyo sababta
  • Hubi waxqabadka xayaysiiska oo hagaaji miisaaniyadda
  • Baadh 2–3 alaab oo cusub
  • Ka jawaab faallooyinka oo maamul soo celinta

MonthlyBil kasta

  • Review overall profit vs. spend
  • Evaluate the supplier relationship — pricing, speed, quality
  • Plan next month's content calendar
  • Dib u eeg faa'iidada guud iyo kharashka
  • Qiimee xiriirka alaab-siiyaha — qiimaha, xawaaraha, tayada
  • Qorshee jadwalka waxqabadka bisha soo socota
