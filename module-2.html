The five building blocks you needShanta walxood ee aad u baahan tahay

  1. A niche — a focused product category rather than "everything."
  2. A supplier — a factory, wholesaler, or platform (AliExpress, CJdropshipping, local wholesaler) that ships when you order.
  3. A storefront — a website or a social media page/WhatsApp catalog where customers order.
  4. A payment method — a way for customers to pay you, and for you to pay suppliers.
  5. Marketing — ads, content, or word of mouth that brings customers in. Without this, nothing else matters.
  1. Niche (qayb gaar ah) — nooc alaab oo la xaqiijiyay, ma aha "wax walba."
  2. Alaab-siiye — warshad, tuujaar, ama barxad (AliExpress, CJdropshipping, tuujaar maxalli ah) oo kuu diri karta marka aad dalbato.
  3. Dukaan — website ama bog bulsho/WhatsApp oo macaamiishu ka dalban karaan.
  4. Hab lacag-bixin — hab macaamiishu kuugu bixiyaan, iyo hab aad u bixiso alaab-siiyaha.
  5. Suuqgeyn — xayaysiis, waxqabad, ama af-ka-af oo macaamiil keena. La'aantiisa, wax kale ma qiimo lahan.
