What is dropshipping?Waa maxay dropshipping-ku?

Dropshipping is a way of selling products online without ever holding the inventory yourself. You run the online store and handle marketing and customer service. When a customer buys something, you forward that order to a supplier, who packs and ships it directly to the customer — never passing through your hands.

Dropshipping waa hab lagu iibiyo alaabta internetka iyadoon aadan marnaba haysan bakhaarka. Waxaad maamashaa dukaanka online-ka ah, waxaadna maamashaa suuqgeynta iyo adeegga macaamiisha. Marka macmiil wax iibsado, dalabka waxaad u gudbisaa alaab-siiyaha, kaas oo si toos ah ugu diri macmiilka — mar walba iyada oo aan gacantaada marin.

You make your profit from the difference between what the customer pays you and what the supplier charges you.

Faa'iidadaada waxay ka timaadaa farqiga u dhexeeya waxa macmiilku ku bixiyo iyo waxa alaab-siiyuhu kaa qaado.

In one sentence:Hal jumlad: you are the storefront and the marketer; the supplier is the warehouse and the delivery service. adigu waxaad tahay dukaanka iyo suuqgeeyaha; alaab-siiyuhuna waa bakhaarka iyo adeegga gaarsiinta.

Why people choose this modelSababta dadku u doortaan qaabkan

  • Low starting cost — no need to buy stock upfront or rent a warehouse.
  • Low risk — you only pay the supplier after a customer has already paid you.
  • Flexible — can be run from a phone or laptop, from anywhere.
  • Wide product range — you can test many products quickly without owning any of them.
  • Kharash bilow oo hooseeya — uma baahnid inaad hore u iibsato alaab ama aad kiraysato bakhaar.
  • Khatar yar — alaab-siiyaha kuma bixisid ilaa macmiilku horta ku bixiyo.
  • Dabacsanaan — waxaad ka shaqayn kartaa taleefan ama laptop, meel kastaba.
  • Alaab kala duwan oo badan — waxaad si degdeg ah u tijaabin kartaa alaab badan iyadoon mid ka mid ahi kuu lahayn.

What it is notWaxa uusan ahayn

  • It is not passive income — running ads, answering customers, and managing suppliers is real, ongoing work.
  • It is not guaranteed profit — margins per item are usually thin, so volume and repeat customers matter.
  • It is not instant — building trust and a reliable supplier chain takes weeks to months.
  • Maahan dakhli aan shaqo lahayn — xayaysiisyada, ka jawaabidda macaamiisha, iyo maaraynta alaab-siiyayaasha waa shaqo dhab ah oo joogto ah.
  • Maahan faa'iido la hubo — faraqa alaab kastaa inta badan waa yar yahay, marka tirada iyo macaamiisha soo noqnoqda ayaa muhiim ah.
  • Maahan mid degdeg ah — dhisidda kalsoonida iyo silsilad alaab-siiye la isku halayn karo waxay qaadataa toddobaadyo ilaa bilo.
